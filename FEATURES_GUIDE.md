## 🛍️ **MY LIST FEATURE** (Available on All Gallery Pages)

### What is "Add to My List"?
A personal wishlist/favorites feature exclusive to logged-in users.

### How to Use:
1. Browse any gallery (Sangeet, Mehendi, Haldi, Vivaha designs)
2. Click on a design to view it in fullscreen
3. Click **"➕ Add to My List"** button
4. Design is saved to your personal collection

### Where to View Your List:
- Check cart count indicator in header
- Use My List section in dashboard
